Jiro — Deal with Confidence

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Jiro is a multilingual used-items marketplace where buyers pay in Pi into escrow. This Privacy Policy explains what data Jiro collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Jiro, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Jiro is operated as a Staging (Testnet) application on the Pi Network for the Pi Next hackathon. The application is currently a demonstration build; payments are simulated on Testnet and no real Pi is moved until the production release.

2. Data we collect

  • Pi identity: your Pi username, verified through Sign in with Pi. Jiro offers no username-and-password accounts; Pi is the only sign-in method.
  • Profile and preferences: display name, language, country and governorate/city, and product interests you choose so listings and suggestions can be matched to you.
  • Listings: product titles, descriptions, condition details, defects, photos, videos, specifications (brand, model, capacity, colour), price in Pi, and location.
  • Orders and escrow: order details, shipping information, tracking status, payment references, inspection confirmations, and dispute evidence (photos and messages you submit).
  • Messages and ratings: buyer–seller chat messages, questions on listings, and two-way ratings.
  • Device and usage data: basic technical information needed to run the app (such as language and region settings). The static demo build stores your session data in your own browser (local storage) on your device.

3. How we use your data

  • To operate the marketplace: show listings, process escrow payments, track shipping, and release or refund funds.
  • To run arbitration: support staff review dispute evidence to decide who is right, within 72 hours.
  • To keep the marketplace safe: verify sellers, enforce the 200π listing cap for unverified sellers, and review reported listings and users.
  • To improve the service: understand which categories and features are used, and target suggestions such as “You may like”.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not show your email address, phone number, or identity documents to other users. Verification documents are visible only to authorised support staff.
  • We do not issue any token of our own. All transactions are in Pi only.

5. Data sharing

Your public profile information (display name, ratings, seriousness percentage, and tier) and your listings are visible to other users, as a marketplace requires. Order and dispute details are visible only to the buyer, the seller, the shipping company where needed, and authorised support staff. We share data with the Pi Network only as required to sign you in and process Pi payments, and with a shipping company only to deliver your order.

6. Data retention

Order, payment, and dispute records are kept while your account is active and for as long as needed to resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and keep financial records. You may ask for your account and personal data to be deleted by contacting support; records that must be kept for legal or dispute reasons are retained in anonymised form where possible.

7. Security

Sign-in is only through Pi. Passwords are not used or stored. In the full version, Pi identity is verified server-side against the Pi Network, payments are completed only through the official Pi payment flow, and access to administrative functions is restricted to authorised staff. The static demo build clearly labels where server-side verification is not yet available.

8. Children

Jiro is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent for optional features such as interest-based suggestions, by contacting support through the app (Help → Contact Us).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app moves from Staging to Production. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact

For any privacy question, contact Jiro support from inside the app (Help → Contact Us).
