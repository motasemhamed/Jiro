These Terms of Service govern your use of Jiro, a multilingual used-items marketplace where buyers pay in Pi into escrow. By signing in with Pi and using Jiro, you agree to these terms.

1. The service

Jiro connects buyers and sellers of used and new items. Jiro acts as a trusted mediator: the buyer’s payment is held in escrow in the app wallet until the deal is completed as agreed, then released to the seller. The app is currently a Staging (Testnet) demonstration for the Pi Next hackathon; payments are simulated on Testnet and no real Pi is moved until the production release.

2. Accounts

  • Sign-in is only through Sign in with Pi. There are no username-and-password accounts.
  • You must be 18 years or older to use Jiro.
  • You are responsible for your Pi account and for everything done under it. If you act as a seller, a buyer, or an administrator, you must use only the role assigned to you.
  • Unverified sellers may list items up to a total of 200π. Higher-value listings require identity verification (ID and selfie) approved by support.

3. Buying and escrow

  • All prices and payments are in Pi only. No other currency or token is accepted.
  • At checkout the buyer pays: the item price, the shipping cost, any applicable taxes, and the app commission. The commission is 5% added to the seller’s price plus a further 5% buyer fee, shown itemised before payment.
  • The payment is held in escrow. It is released to the seller only after both the buyer confirms receipt and the shipping company’s tracking confirms delivery.
  • After delivery the buyer has a 5-day inspection period. If the buyer neither confirms nor opens a dispute within that period, the funds are released to the seller automatically, and the buyer is notified of the remaining time and the exact release date and time.
  • The buyer pays the shipping cost. Local pickup with inspection is available where the seller offers it; escrow is released instantly on pickup confirmation.

4. Selling

  • Every listing must have a real photo of the actual item. Listings without a photo cannot be published.
  • The seller must describe the item’s condition fully and honestly, including every defect, anything broken or damaged, and anything needing repair or replacement, with real photos and a video of the item and its condition. For phones and devices, a diagnostic report should be added where available.
  • The seller must ship with tracked delivery through a shipping company with tracking visible to both parties, and provide a packing video.
  • Stolen goods, counterfeit goods, and prohibited items are not allowed. Jiro may remove any listing and suspend any account that violates this rule.
  • The seller may set any price; there is no minimum or maximum. New items must be priced higher than the equivalent used item.

5. Inspection, disputes, and arbitration

  • On receipt, the buyer compares the item with the seller’s description and video. If it matches, the buyer presses “Receive” and the funds are released.
  • If it does not match, the buyer may request a refund and open a dispute, photographing the differences from what the seller described.
  • A dispute freezes the funds. Arbitration is carried out by platform staff whose only job is deciding disputes, with strict neutrality. The arbitrator may request additional evidence from either party.
  • Arbitration decisions are made within 72 hours (3 working days) and are final and binding on both parties under these terms.
  • If arbitration favours the buyer, the item is returned and the return shipping cost is deducted from the seller; the buyer is refunded (in full, or partially where a partial refund is decided). If arbitration favours the seller, the buyer must accept the item and the funds are released to the seller immediately.

6. Shipping insurance

Optional shipping insurance covers loss or damage up to 50π per shipment, at a premium of 2% of the covered value. Claims are filed in the app and reviewed by support; compensation is paid up to the covered amount.

7. Fees, offers, and auctions

Offers and counter-offers are binding once accepted. Auction bids are binding; the winning bid is paid into escrow. Featured listing promotion is a paid service in Pi. All fees are shown before you confirm any payment.

8. Ratings and conduct

Ratings are two-way: buyers rate the seller and the product, sellers rate the buyer. A seriousness percentage is calculated from dispute-free successful deals, and users are classified as distinguished, excellent, good, hesitant, or having a dispute history. You must not manipulate ratings, misrepresent items, or attempt to complete deals outside Jiro to avoid the commission.

9. Prohibited conduct

You must not list stolen, counterfeit, or illegal items; post false information; harass other users; attempt to access another user’s account or administrative functions; or use Jiro for any unlawful purpose. Violations may lead to removal of listings, suspension, or a permanent ban.

Jiro is not the seller of any item and does not inspect items itself; the shipping company proves delivery only and does not inspect the item. To the extent permitted by law, Jiro’s liability is limited to the amount held in escrow for the order in question. The Staging demo is provided “as is” for testing and evaluation.
